Meta's promise ledger, re-scored: what Muse announced, what shipped, and what is still waiting

The October 20 ledger scored six Muse promises against what had shipped. Two weeks on, voice arrived with no announcement, sharing came back in a new form nobody has sighted in the app, and Japan and Singapore launched with partners that are only partly live. Wonder in the app, a Windows app, and the full Wonder safety report have not moved at all.

By Marvin Jones

Last checked against the live product: November 2, 2026.

Promise Ledger, scored November 3, 2026

Promised, with the date it was promised:

  • Realtime voice in the app: presented at Connect, no rollout date given.
  • Share links for artifacts: live at launch, then paused on October 20, 2026 after Claroc researchers found more than 6,800 Muse share addresses in public DNS records.
  • Muse in Japan and Singapore: launched in the United States on September 8, 2026 and in Canada on September 18, with more countries promised and none dated at launch.
  • Muse Wonder inside the Muse app: released for developers on October 12, 2026, with the app version described by Meta as rolling out first to subscribers. No date.
  • Wonder's fuller safety report: Meta said a fuller report would follow the initial release.
  • A Confidential VM, the setup where Meta itself cannot see a user's data: described at launch as coming later this year, with trusted testers first.
  • A native Windows app: browser only at launch, no date.
  • 1Password secure credential storage: announced, with the product page still carrying coming soon wording weeks after launch.
  • Muse on Meta's glasses: promised at Connect for the coming months, no date at the time.

Shipped, with the check that supports it:

  • Realtime voice: reported rolling out on October 24, 2026 by TestingCatalog, via All Things Muse (October 25). Single chain, no Meta announcement. Nothing dated November 1 or 2 in any sweep since.
  • Share-link sharing: restored on October 22, 2026, per The Verge and ExtremeTech coverage of Meta's action. Artifacts made before October 22 got new URLs, and old links now tell a visitor to get the new link from the sharer.
  • Japan: launched October 23, 2026, with Mercari, Money Forward ME, and Ticket Pia named as partners.
  • Singapore: launched October 27, 2026, with eight partners named. Agoda is confirmed live per partner press. The other seven were described in local coverage as connecting progressively.
  • Money Forward ME in Japan: live per vendor. Its support site said on October 24, 2026 that a connector for use in Muse had started, and Money Forward Corp published its press release on October 26. No in-app sighting from a Japan account has appeared in any sweep.

Still waiting, checked again on November 2, 2026:

  • Wonder inside the Muse app: no in-app confirmation in any November 2 sweep. Meta's official wording is still will soon power and roll out first to subscribers.
  • Wonder's fuller safety report: still forthcoming. No publication date has been given.
  • Confidential VM: still a single-outlet TestingCatalog sighting of a web settings section, plus back-end preparation talk. No second source, and no Meta date.
  • A native Windows app: no installer and no date. Sweeps on November 2 found Charm items and third-party pages only, and the browser remains the way to use Muse on Windows.
  • 1Password secure credential storage: announced and undated. No live date in any sweep checked for this post.
  • Muse on glasses: not shipped in any item dated November 1 or 2. This one did gain a window, covered below.
  • Mercari and Ticket Pia in Japan: not live, no date. Mercari's next check is its own results day, Thursday, November 12, 2026.
  • Charm, Meta's wearable: still December in the coverage, with no official price.

Next re-score: after Mercari's November 12 results, and again when Wonder is sighted in the app. The re-score runs even if the answer is still nothing moved.

The short version

On October 20, the ledger was mostly a waiting list. It still is. Two of the items that moved did so quietly. Voice showed up in accounts on October 24 with no post from Meta. Sharing returned on October 22 in a new URL form that, as of November 2, no sweep has actually sighted inside an app.

The loud moves were the country launches. Japan on October 23 and Singapore on October 27 took Muse from two countries to four. Inside both launches, named did not mean live. Japan has one connector live per its vendor and two still waiting. Singapore has one partner confirmed live and seven described as progressive.

The core promises readers ask about most have the least movement. Wonder in the personal app, the fuller safety report, and the Confidential VM read today almost exactly as they read on October 20. Two weeks of checking produced no new date for any of them.

Voice arrived without an announcement

Realtime voice is the cleanest shipped item in this ledger, and it is still a thin record. TestingCatalog reported on October 24, 2026 that realtime voice conversations had begun rolling out to Muse users. All Things Muse carried the report on October 25. A user saw the feature appear. Meta published no announcement that any sweep has found.

That pattern matters for how readers should hold the claim. It is scored shipped because a named testing outlet reported a rollout with a date, not because Meta confirmed one. No second sighting and no wider rollout date appeared on October 31, November 1, or November 2. Screen viewing and web voice calls showed up in the same period as preparation items only, with no date attached. Preparation is not a rollout, and this ledger does not score it as one.

Sharing came back in a form nobody has sighted

The share-link story moved twice in three days, and then stopped. On October 20, Claroc's finding became public: more than 6,800 Muse share links sat in public DNS records, readable by anyone who looked. Meta paused sharing that day. On October 22, sharing was restored with new URLs. Pre-October 22 artifacts received new addresses, and an old link now shows a note asking the visitor to get the new link from the person who shared it.

That restoration is third-party coverage of Meta's action, in The Verge and ExtremeTech among others. Today's DuckDuckGo check found no Meta announcement of the restoration. More to the point for a user, no sweep from October 22 through November 2 found a first-person sighting of the new link format inside the app. Restored, per coverage. Sighted in use, not yet. Those two lines stay separate in the box at the top of this page for a reason.

Caution

A restored feature and a confirmed rollout are different things, and this ledger has now scored one of each in the same post. Voice was sighted in an account and never announced. Sharing was announced by coverage and never sighted. If a claim about Muse matters to you, ask which of those two kinds of evidence sits under it before you repeat it.

Two countries launched. Their partners mostly did not.

Japan launched on October 23, 2026 (https://museexplained.com/guide/muse-japan-launch/). Meta named Mercari, Money Forward ME, and Ticket Pia that day. Money Forward ME moved first: its support site said on October 24 that its connector had started, and its press release followed on October 26, with a read-only connection and no financial institution credentials shared with Muse. Checked again on November 2, Mercari and Ticket Pia still had not moved. The November 1 post on this site corrected Mercari's next check date to November 12, on Mercari's own investor relations announcement, after earnings calendars had carried November 4 (https://museexplained.com/guide/mercaris-turn-japan-second-connector/).

Singapore launched on October 27, 2026 (https://museexplained.com/guide/muse-singapore-launch/). Eight partners were named: Shopee, Grab, Singapore Airlines, Agoda, ShopBack, Klook, Trip.com, and Lazada. Only Agoda is confirmed live. The rest are progressive, the word local coverage used, which means arriving later in an order nobody has published.

Two price and place facts around these launches stay unresolved, and this post will not smooth them over. Japan's yen prices conflict: one in-app report carried 3,499 yen for Power and 16,990 yen for Maximum, while launch coverage in Impress and PC Watch, via SmartScope, listed 2,799 yen and 13,990 yen. Neither set is printed here as settled. Singapore's subscription range, S$22 to S$139 a month from finance.biggo.com, is still single-source and not confirmed by Meta. A later snippet carried S$27.98 and S$139.98, which does not settle it either. And Kuwait, which has appeared as a rumored fifth country, is not confirmed. Every four-country list in the November 2 sweeps contradicts it.

Wonder has not reached the app, and its safety report has not reached anyone

This is the line readers of the Wonder rollout watch already know (https://museexplained.com/guide/muse-wonder-app-rollout/). Wonder and Wonder Full were released on October 12, 2026 for developers, through the API and Muse Code. For the personal agent app, Meta's wording has not changed in any source checked through November 2: Wonder will soon power the app, and it will roll out first to subscribers.

Soon is now three weeks old. No November 1 or 2 sweep found an in-app confirmation. Two weaker items sit in the record and neither is counted here. SmartScope reported on October 26 that a Japan app upgrade screen carried a Runs on Muse Wonder note, uncorroborated. One low-authority claim says subscribers already have Wonder in the app, and it conflicts with Meta's own wording, so the ledger leaves it out rather than averaging the two.

The safety report is in the same state. Meta said it would publish a fuller report following the initial release. As of November 2, that report is still forthcoming, with no date. A promise to publish is the only evidence that exists for it, so the ledger scores the promise, not the report.

Field note

There is one honest use for a ledger like this, and it is not prediction. On October 20, a reader could not tell from Meta's own pages which of these items had dates and which had only adjectives. Writing the adjectives down, with the date they were checked, is what lets a reader in December see whether soon meant three weeks or three months. The answer so far, for Wonder in the app, is at least three weeks and counting.

The rest of the waiting list

Confidential VM is the privacy promise underneath Muse. Today, per Meta's own security writing quoted in the September baseline research for this site, policies restrict who at Meta can reach a user's data, but nothing cryptographic prevents it. The Confidential VM is the version where Meta cannot see the data. At launch it was described as coming later this year, with trusted testers first. Since then, the only addition in the record is a single-outlet TestingCatalog sighting of a settings section in the web interface, with no second source and no date from Meta. Later this year is a narrowing window. It has not narrowed to a day.

A native Windows app has not appeared. There is no installer to download and no date to wait for, and the November 2 sweeps on the subject returned Charm coverage and third-party pages. Readers searching for a Muse Windows app release date are searching for a fact that does not exist yet in any source this site has checked.

1Password secure credential storage was announced as the answer to a real friction point: an agent that books and buys things needs credentials, and typing your own passwords into it defeats the purpose. Weeks after launch, the product page still carried coming soon wording, and no sweep for this post found a live date. Announced, undated, still waiting.

Muse on glasses is the one waiting item that gained something. On October 21, Engadget reported the Q3 earnings call: Zuckerberg said Muse will be available on Meta's glasses next month, meaning November 2026, and Muse is central to the glasses business model. Engadget also noted that at least some Muse features on glasses may be paywalled. That paywall point is Engadget's reading of his subscription remarks, not a Meta price announcement, and it is labeled that way here. November has started. No item dated November 1 or 2 shows Muse live on glasses. The window is open, and the check is now dated in a way it was not on October 20.

Charm, the wearable, stays where it was: December in the coverage, no official price. A 349 dollar figure appeared in November 1 snippets and conflicts with the earlier finding that no official price exists, so it is not banked here as a price.

Spark Open, for readers tracking the open model: the last count this site banked was 7,342 downloads in the last month on Hugging Face, viewed October 26, 2026. No November 1 or 2 sweep refreshed that number, and no verified community local run has surfaced. The score is no newer count, not a new total.

The fixed frame

Two things have not moved at all, and they frame everything above. United States pricing was flat in every November 2 sweep. Free gets 100 million tokens a week. Power is 20 dollars a month, with 500 million a week. Maximum is 100 dollars a month, with 3 billion a week. No published extra-token price has surfaced.

The country list is also flat, at four: the United States (September 8, 2026), Canada (September 18), Japan (October 23), and Singapore (October 27). Four launches in seven weeks is fast. It is also the whole list, whatever a rumored fifth country says.

Which promised Muse feature are you still waiting for, and have you seen any sign of it in your app? That is the reader question for this re-score. Send a dated in-app sighting: the feature, your account tier, and the date you saw it. A sighting that meets that bar feeds the next re-score, and a partner list or a launch recap does not. The next re-score runs after November 12, and again when Wonder is sighted in the app, even if the answer is still nothing moved.

Quick answers

Has Muse Wonder reached the Muse app?

No, not in any source checked through November 2, 2026. Wonder is live for developers through the API and Muse Code since October 12. For the personal app, Meta's wording is still will soon power and roll out first to subscribers, and no in-app confirmation appeared in any November 2 sweep.

What shipped since the October 20 ledger?

Realtime voice, reported rolling out on October 24 by TestingCatalog with no Meta announcement. Share-link sharing, restored October 22 with new URLs after the October 20 pause. And two country launches: Japan on October 23 and Singapore on October 27. Each has only one partner confirmed live so far. That is Money Forward ME in Japan, per its vendor, and Agoda in Singapore, per partner press.

Is there a Muse app for Windows?

No native app that any source checked for this post could point to, and no release date. The browser is still the way to use Muse on Windows as of November 2, 2026.

When is Muse coming to Meta's glasses?

November 2026 is the window Engadget reported from the October 21 Q3 call, where Zuckerberg said next month. Nothing dated November 1 or 2 shows it live yet. There is no specific day.

What happened to the Confidential VM?

Nothing dated. It was described at launch as coming later this year with trusted testers first. The only later item is a single-outlet TestingCatalog sighting of a web settings section. No second source and no Meta date had appeared by November 2.

Has Wonder's full safety report been published?

No. Meta said a fuller report would follow the initial October 12 release. As of November 2 it is still forthcoming, with no publication date given.

How many countries have Muse now?

Four, as of November 2, 2026: the United States, Canada, Japan, and Singapore. Kuwait is not confirmed, and four-country lists in the November 2 sweeps contradict it.

Did Muse prices change?

No. Every November 2 sweep held the United States tiers flat: free at 100 million tokens a week, Power at 20 dollars a month and 500 million a week, and Maximum at 100 dollars a month and 3 billion a week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why score voice as shipped if Meta never announced it?

A: Because the evidence standard is a dated report of the thing itself, not a press release. TestingCatalog reported the rollout on October 24, 2026 and a user saw the feature appear. The ledger labels it single chain and no Meta announcement, in the box and in the text, so a reader can weigh it accordingly. It does not label it confirmed by Meta.

Q: Why is share-link sharing scored shipped if nobody has sighted the new format in the app?

A: It is scored restored, on third-party coverage dated October 22, 2026 in The Verge, ExtremeTech, and others reporting Meta's action, with new URLs for older artifacts and a notice on old links. The in-app sighting is recorded separately, as missing. Both facts are in the ledger because both are true, and keeping them apart is the point of scoring it this way.

Q: Is 1Password live in Muse?

A: No live date has appeared in any sweep checked for this post. Secure credential storage through 1Password was announced, the product page carried coming soon wording weeks after launch, and the status on November 2 is announced and undated.

Q: Could Wonder already be in my app without a label saying so?

A: It could, and that possibility is why the standard here is a sighting, not an inference. An upgrade screen label or a dated first-person report of Wonder in the app would move this ledger. A faster answer or a better result would not, because both are also consistent with the Spark model the app has run on. Until a sighting appears, the status stays not confirmed.

Q: What is the next dated check on this ledger?

A: Thursday, November 12, 2026, when Mercari publishes its first quarter results, per Mercari's own investor relations announcement. It is a check date for any Muse mention in those results, not a promised connector launch. The glasses window, November, is the other live check this month, with no specific day attached.

Q: Will this ledger be updated again?

A: Yes. After November 12, and again when Wonder is sighted in the app. The update runs even if nothing has moved, because a dated nothing is still a finding a reader can use.
