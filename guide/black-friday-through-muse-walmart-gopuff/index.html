Black Friday Through Muse: Did Anyone Actually Buy at Walmart or Gopuff, and What Did the Receipt Show?

Walmart and Gopuff were both reported live through Muse on November 24, 2026. DuckDuckGo searches on November 26 found no named first purchase, no receipt, and no completed Gopuff delivery. Walmart did publish its own post on November 24, and its help page sets out the per order limit and the approval step in Walmart's own words.

By Marvin Jones

Last checked against the live product: November 26, 2026.

No verified purchase was found. That is the result of the November 26 check, and I am putting it in the first screen instead of burying it under launch copy. Walmart through Muse is reported live. Gopuff through Muse is reported live. On the record read on November 26, 2026, no named shopper had completed a Walmart order through Muse, no receipt had been published, and no Gopuff order through Muse had been reported delivered.

Promise Ledger

This post will be checked again on December 4, 2026, or sooner if a named purchase report or a retailer primary lands first, for two rows:

  • Row 1, Walmart: open. Walmart's Agent Gateway was reported live by The Verge on November 23, 2026, and Walmart published its own corporate post on November 24, 2026. No first verified user purchase was found on November 26, 2026.
  • Row 2, Gopuff: open. The Gopuff integration was announced on November 24, 2026 at 8:00 AM EST, which is 7:00 AM CST, by Fox Business and by BusinessWire. No completed Gopuff order through Muse was found on November 26, 2026.

Each row closes only on a named source: a shopper who names the item, the total, and the approval step, or a Walmart, Gopuff, or Meta page that reports a completed order. A launch announcement does not close either row.

What the November 26 search actually covered

DuckDuckGo searches on November 26, 2026, at about 10:05 AM CST, covered five angles: a completed Walmart purchase and user report, a completed Gopuff order, the Agent Gateway and any receipt or spending limit report, Black Friday shopping through Muse at either retailer, and Walmart and Meta primary pages.

The November 26 items that came back were secondary coverage of the November 24 Walmart announcement. PaySpace Magazine, dated November 26, 2026 at 10:34 AM with no timezone shown, restated the launch and its controls. The Seoul Economic Daily, dated November 26, 2026 at 05:44:12 with no timezone shown, cited TechCrunch reporting and named no shopper. A roundup dated November 27, 2026, with a footer stating its news was checked on November 26, reported no new purchase either. None of those items names a buyer, an item bought through Muse, an order total, or a delivery.

Field note

The search ran on November 26, the day before Black Friday itself, which falls on November 27, 2026. A purchase made on Black Friday morning cannot appear in a November 26 search. That timing is a limit on this post, and it is why the December 4 check date is printed above instead of a final verdict.

Walmart: a primary exists, a purchase does not

One part of this site's earlier record changed on November 26. Through November 25, no Walmart or Meta primary for the Agent Gateway had been banked here. On November 26, Walmart's corporate post was read in full: Walmart's Black Friday Deals Continue. Meta's Muse Can Now Shop Them, Too, dated November 24, 2026, with no time shown, on corporate.walmart.com.

Walmart's post says, starting today, customers can shop Walmart's Black Friday deals directly through Muse, powered by the Walmart Agent Gateway. It describes Agent Profiles as a place where a customer can set spending limits, approve purchases, and check purchase history. That is Walmart speaking about its own flow, and it moves the launch record from secondhand reporting to a retailer primary.

The Verge reported the flow on November 23, 2026. TechCrunch, by Lauren Forristal on November 24, 2026 at 1:32 PM PST, which is 3:32 PM CST, reported it before Walmart's post was banked here. The flow is the same in both: sign in to Walmart in the Muse app, find, compare, and purchase Black Friday deals, with spending limits and a deletable Agent Profile. TechCrunch adds two limits worth keeping: Muse gets no access to Walmart purchase history, and the Agent Gateway is a Model Context Protocol server that Walmart plans to open to more developers and platforms. Instacart is also named as a Muse partner in the TechCrunch report. Amazon is contrasted in both reports as blocking some third party shopping agents.

No Walmart post, and no report read on November 26, names a completed purchase. Reported live is not used live, and this post keeps those two records apart.

The Walmart limit and approval step, in Walmart's own help page

Walmart's help page, titled Shopping with Walmart and Muse, read on November 26, 2026 with no date shown on the page, gives the control in specific terms. A customer sets a per order spending limit when first setting up an Agent Profile. Checkout completes in Muse with a card saved in the customer's Walmart account, at Walmart.com prices. If an order total is above the spending limit, Walmart asks the customer to approve that order. That approval applies only to that order. In some cases Walmart may send a separate approval request by email or push notification. A confirmation appears in Muse after an order is placed, per the same help page.

That is a description of how the control is built. It is not a report that the control fired in a real Black Friday checkout. No source read on November 26 shows a limit stopping an order, an approval request arriving, or a confirmation screen from a named account.

Caution

Walmart's Agent Gateway page on walmart.com, read on November 26, 2026 with no date shown, describes the Gateway as an early beta and invitation only, alongside the line about setting agent spending limits and which purchases need approval. Walmart's corporate post says customers can shop starting November 24. Those two pages sit side by side without explaining who is invited and who is not, so a reader whose account shows no Walmart option in Muse is not necessarily doing anything wrong. One Chinese morning brief, Ebrun, dated November 26, 2026 at 06:31 with no timezone shown, reported a spending cap range of $50 to $200. No Walmart page read on November 26 states that range, so I am not printing it as a Walmart rule.

What Walmart still has not answered

The November 23 and November 24 record settles the saved card, the limit, the approval step, the price basis, and returns through Walmart's normal policy, per The Verge report. It does not settle whether Walmart Plus benefits, coupons or promo codes, gift cards, or split payment work in an agent checkout. No Walmart primary read on November 26 answers any of those four, so each one stays not confirmed in this post. Do not assume a gift card works because a saved card does.

Gopuff: an announcement, no delivery

Fox Business, by Emma Bussey, published November 24, 2026 at 8:00 AM EST, and BusinessWire the same morning, announced a direct Gopuff integration in Muse. The facts in those reports are these: more than 5,000 products, delivery in as fast as 15 minutes, a link through Settings, then Connectors, or by typing Connect to Gopuff, stock and estimated arrival shown in Muse, approval asked before checkout, and free delivery on the first three orders for new linked users. BusinessWire carries a 99.5 percent order accuracy figure, and that figure is Gopuff's own claim, printed here as a claim.

As fast as 15 minutes is a promised speed. No report read on November 26 gives an actual delivery time for an order placed through Muse, a street address it reached, or a total on a Gopuff receipt through Muse. Coverage is the other open part: neither November 24 report states which cities the Muse flow serves, and neither states the fees or minimums after the first three free deliveries. Both stay unconfirmed.

Numbers from before Black Friday are not Black Friday results

Coverage before November 27 carried shopping figures for Muse from earlier in November, including an agentic orders share attributed to an analytics firm. A November 27 dated roundup repeated a 51 percent figure and, to its credit, said the firm had not published its method. Whatever that figure measures, its window ended before Black Friday. It cannot tell a reader whether a Walmart order completed on November 27, what a receipt showed, or how long a Gopuff delivery took, so it does no work in this post beyond this paragraph.

The Amazon record is separate too. Reports this week described talks involving Amazon access, with no deal and no flow a shopper can test. There is no Amazon checkout through Muse to check on Black Friday, and this post does not treat the Walmart and Gopuff flows as evidence about Amazon in either direction.

The question for readers

If you bought through Muse at Walmart or Gopuff today, what limit or approval step appeared before checkout, and would you send the receipt? An answer that names the retailer, the item, the order total, and whether an approval request arrived by email or push notification will anchor the December 4, 2026 follow up. Names will be withheld unless you say to print yours.

Quick answers

Did anyone actually buy at Walmart through Muse?

No verified purchase was found on November 26, 2026. DuckDuckGo searches that day found launch coverage from November 23 and November 24 and secondary repeats on November 26, with no named shopper, item, or receipt.

Did anyone complete a Gopuff order through Muse?

No completed order was found on November 26, 2026. The November 24 announcement describes the flow. No delivery through Muse was reported in the sources read that day.

Is there a Walmart primary for the Agent Gateway?

Yes, banked on November 26, 2026. Walmart's corporate post, dated November 24, 2026, says customers can shop its Black Friday deals directly through Muse, powered by the Walmart Agent Gateway, with Agent Profiles for spending limits, approvals, and purchase history.

How does the Walmart spending limit work?

Per Walmart's help page, you set a per order limit when you first set up an Agent Profile. An order above that limit needs your approval, that approval applies only to that order, and Walmart may in some cases send a separate approval request by email or push notification.

What card does a Walmart purchase through Muse charge?

A card saved in your Walmart account, per Walmart's corporate post and the reports from The Verge and TechCrunch. Checkout completes in the Muse conversation, at Walmart.com prices.

How fast is Gopuff through Muse?

The announced figure is delivery in as fast as 15 minutes, per Fox Business and BusinessWire on November 24, 2026. No actual delivery time for an order placed through Muse was reported in the sources read on November 26.

Can I use a Walmart gift card or a coupon through Muse?

Not confirmed. No Walmart primary read on November 26, 2026 states whether Walmart Plus benefits, coupons, gift cards, or split payment work in an agent checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When will the first verified purchase be reported here?

A: This post will be checked again on December 4, 2026, or sooner if a named purchase report or a retailer primary lands first. A row in the Promise Ledger closes only on a named shopper or a Walmart, Gopuff, or Meta page reporting a completed order.

Q: Does Muse see my Walmart purchase history?

A: No, per TechCrunch, November 24, 2026 at 1:32 PM PST. Walmart's corporate post says an Agent Profile lets the customer check purchase history. Those are two different statements about two different parties, and both are printed here as reported.

Q: Can I delete the Walmart Agent Profile?

A: Yes, per The Verge, November 23, 2026, and TechCrunch, November 24, 2026. The profile is deletable, and spending limits and approval settings sit inside it.

Q: How do I link Gopuff in Muse?

A: Through Settings, then Connectors, or by typing Connect to Gopuff, per Fox Business and BusinessWire, November 24, 2026. New linked users get free delivery on the first three orders, per the same reports. Fees after those three orders were not stated in either report.

Q: Is the Walmart flow open to everyone?

A: Not on the full record. Walmart's corporate post says customers can shop starting November 24, 2026, while Walmart's Agent Gateway page describes an early beta that is invitation only. No source read on November 26 reconciles those two statements.

Q: Did Meta publish its own post about Walmart or Gopuff shopping?

A: No Meta primary about Walmart or Gopuff shopping through Muse was found in the November 26, 2026 searches. The retailer primaries banked are Walmart's corporate post for Walmart, and the BusinessWire release for Gopuff.
