What's stored, what's deleted: Muse and your data

Deleting a chat does not delete what it learned. Meta's own help page says so. What lives where, what the three deletion controls actually do, and the quiet ways your data survives.

By Marvin Jones

Last checked against the live product: October 9, 2026.

This is the post Meta's own documentation writes for me. Buried in Meta's "How to manage your Muse data" help page is this sentence, verbatim: "After you delete something from Muse, Muse may still remember information it learned from what you deleted."

Read that again. Delete the message, keep the memory. That is not a bug report. It is the design, stated openly. What follows is the honest map of what Muse keeps, where it keeps it, and what each delete button actually deletes.

What Meta says Muse stores

Meta's help page lists five things your Muse keeps: System Files, Reminders and scheduled tasks, Memories (in a file called Memory.md, which you can open, edit, and delete directly), Soul, and Identity.

The three systems, and why one button is not enough

Your data lives in (at least) three separate places, and each one has its own control: 1. The stored stuff (files, chats, reminders — what delete and reset touch). 2. The memory (Memory.md and what was learned — deleting a chat removes the record, not necessarily what the agent learned). 3. The training pipeline (your trajectories — sharing is on by default; opt out in Settings, Data controls).

Field note

The practical audit is simple and worth doing monthly: open Memory.md and read it. Delete entries that are stale, wrong, or sensitive. It takes two minutes and it is the one transparency tool in this whole industry that actually works.

What deletion actually requires

Two levels: Reset Muse (Settings, Data controls) wipes your Muse data. Delete your Meta account removes everything. There is no clearly labeled "delete just my Muse, keep my Meta account" path.

Caution

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your data. If you are leaving for real: opt out of training first, clear the Memory file, reset the agent. Meta has published no retention periods for Muse data.

Quick answers

Does deleting a chat delete what Muse learned from it?

Not necessarily. Meta's own help page: "After you delete something from Muse, Muse may still remember information it learned from what you deleted."

Can I stop Muse from training on my conversations?

Yes: Settings, Data controls, "Help improve our AI models," confirm. It is on by default.

Can I turn off memory entirely?

Reviewers who tested it say no. You can edit, wipe, or clear the Memory.md file, but the memory capability itself cannot be switched off.
